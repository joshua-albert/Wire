How corrals work

  • $5 parks you all day during the posted hours. Pay at a pay-by-plate kiosk or in the meterUP app.
  • Zone number: it starts with 99 and is posted at the corral. Enter it when you pay.
  • Permits: $75 for 3 months, $150 for 6, $225 for 9, $250 for a year. From the PPA Permit Office at 35 N. 8th St. Bring your registration.
  • Squeeze in. Dense parking is allowed. Park carefully so you don't knock anyone over.
  • Residential permits don't work in corrals. Don't park in bike corrals.

Locations, sides of the street and hours come from the PPA's official corral map, checked Nov 20, 2026. Pins sit mid-block on the side the PPA lists, so look for the sign. Rules and prices are from philapark.org. Signs on the street always win.
